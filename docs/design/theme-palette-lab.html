Note. This scratchpad is informal and evolving. The spec remains the source of truth when a decision is locked.

1. TLDR

The dashboard should feel calm during long working sessions. Surfaces must separate without shouting, and status colors must remain clear when the content is dense.

  • Keep the paper-like material, but remove glare and muddy low-contrast zones.
  • Keep the same quick scan path from workspace to ticket to comment.
  • Use a restrained accent so comments and status signals retain priority.
